## Bundled pictures

| Picture | Origin | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `contents/images/world.png` | Original artwork in the style of an early X11 workstation, drawn for this wallpaper by `tools/gen_art.py` | GPL-2.0-or-later |
| `contents/images/kde1.png` | 1-bit conversion of [KDE 1.1.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KDE_1.1.jpg), screenshot by the KDE development team | GPL |
| `contents/images/kde2.png` | 1-bit conversion of [KDE 2.2.2.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KDE_2.2.2.png), screenshot by the KDE development team (uploaded by KAMiKAZOW) | GPL |
| `contents/images/kde3.png` | 1-bit conversion of [KDE 3.5 screenshot.png](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:KDE_3.5_screenshot.png), screenshot by Golradir | GPL |

The KDE screenshots were sharpened and reduced to black and white with an
ordered dither by `tools/convert.py`; nothing else was changed.

The text in the retro desktop is set in Terminus Font (SIL Open Font License
1.1), used only to draw the picture; the font itself is not part of the
installed wallpaper.

The terminal in the retro desktop quotes Linus Torvalds.
